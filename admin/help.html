Website Editor Help

Use these steps to update the church website safely.

Publish an update

  1. Open the editor and sign in using your invited account.
  2. Choose Church Updates, Weekly Schedule, Leadership, Ministries, or Contact and Site Details.
  3. Make the change and check spelling, dates, times, and telephone numbers.
  4. Select Save or Publish. The website normally updates after the Netlify build finishes.
  5. Open the public website and confirm the change.

Important safeguards

Only publish confirmed dates, study topics, leadership titles, and event details. Turn an announcement off instead of deleting it when you may need it again.

Uploaded photos should be clear, respectful, and approved for public use.
